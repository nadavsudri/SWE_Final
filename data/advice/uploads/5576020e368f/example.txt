# Example advice for testing the pipeline. One advice per line; lines starting with # are ignored.
Every district should receive at least one new facility.
Give priority to districts where less than 30% of the people are within reach of a facility.
No single district should receive more than a third of the new facilities.
Districts that have no existing facility should be served before others.
More populated districts should get more facilities than small ones.
Prefer locations close to the main roads.
